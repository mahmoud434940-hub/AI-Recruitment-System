AI-powered recruitment automation built with n8n, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Gmail, Gemini AI, and Groq.

Project Overview

This system automates the entire recruitment process from candidate application submission to AI screening and email communication.

Features

Application Form

Candidates submit resumes through an online form.

CV Storage

Resumes are saved automatically to Google Drive.

Google Sheets Tracking

All applicants are logged and tracked automatically.

AI Screening

Gemini and Groq evaluate candidate resumes.

Email Automation

Acceptance and rejection emails are sent automatically.

Error Monitoring

Track workflow failures and ensure reliability.

Technology Stack

n8n Gemini AI Groq Google Sheets Google Drive Gmail ngrok GitHub Pages
